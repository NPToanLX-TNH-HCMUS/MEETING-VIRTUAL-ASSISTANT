# Meeting Minutes — Remote control project: conceptual design
**Date:** 2026-11-16  

## Summary

The team discussed a jog dial to control volume and channels and decided to include one. The team decided to put the company slogan on the remote, for example on the casing. The form and placement of the jog dial and whether the buttons should be flush with the case were left open. The designers will build a prototype for the next meeting.

## Decisions

- The remote will have a jog dial.
- The company slogan will be put on the remote, for example on the casing.
- The materials will be plastic and rubber.
