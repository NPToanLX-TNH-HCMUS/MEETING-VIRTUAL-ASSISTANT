# Meeting Minutes — Remote control project: functional design
**Date:** 2026-11-09  

## Summary

The project manager recapped the kick-off meeting and presented three new requirements from the company: no teletext, the remote only controls the television, and the corporate colour and slogan must be part of the design. The project manager assigned work for the next stage, and the team agreed to group the functions of the remote into the three categories proposed by the industrial designer.

## Decisions

- The remote will not include teletext.
- The remote will only control the television, not a VCR, DVD or other devices.
- The corporate colour and slogan will be incorporated into the design of the remote.
- The functions will be grouped into the three categories proposed by the industrial designer.
