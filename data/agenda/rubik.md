# Meeting Agenda: Rubik's Cube


## Objective
Introduce the Rubik's Cube, walk through a beginner solving method, and look at ways to get faster.

## Details

### 1. Cube basics & notation (10 min)
- Parts of the cube: centers, edges, corners
- Move notation: R, L, U, D, F, B, prime ('), and double (2) moves
- How to hold the cube

### 2. Beginner method (25 min)
- Step 1: White cross
- Step 2: White corners (first layer)
- Step 3: Middle-layer edges
- Step 4: Yellow cross
- Step 5: Orient and permute the last layer

### 3. Speedcubing tips (15 min)
- Introduction to CFOP (Cross, F2L, OLL, PLL)
- Finger tricks and turning efficiency
- Choosing a speed cube; practice routines and timers

### 4. Q&A and wrap-up (10 min)
- Open questions
- Practice goals before the next meeting
